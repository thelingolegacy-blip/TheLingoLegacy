Privacy Policy

This policy covers the public The Lingo Legacy website, That’s My Lingo, Loyalty Lane, KottonsCode, founder pages, and related static launch surfaces.

Information we collect

The launch site is static-first. Most pages do not require an account. If you choose to email us, reserve a founder drop, request access, or contact the studio, we receive the information you send, such as your name, email address, selected tier, and message.

Analytics

We use privacy-minded analytics to understand page visits, traffic sources, device categories, and launch performance. Analytics are used to improve the site and do not create a cash-value wallet or gambling profile.

Children and youth experiences

KottonsCode pages are designed as static, adult-guided story and learning previews. The current site does not require child accounts, child profiles, or cloud saves. Future youth features require privacy and safety review before launch.

Payments and purchases

Checkout, memberships, digital downloads, and apparel sales are only active when routed through approved commerce providers. Do not send payment card details by email or contact forms.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hello@thelingolegacy.com.
