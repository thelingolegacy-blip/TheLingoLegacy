Recovery safety protocol

Every repair follows this order. A reset is not a first response.

1

Identify scope

Exact service, environment, owner, dependency and incident reference.

2

Capture baseline

Commit, config, deployment ID, logs, backup and current health.

3

Classify impact

Security, data integrity, availability, cost and customer exposure.

4

Approve change

Named approver, narrow scope, maintenance window and rollback trigger.

5

Execute and verify

One controlled change; automated checks plus human acceptance.

6

Close with evidence

Correlated artifacts, final status, reviewer and rollback disposition.
