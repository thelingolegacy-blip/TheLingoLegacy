Operating overview

A control surface for work coordination and governance. Live state must come from authenticated, timestamped source integrations—not hard-coded tiles.

Truth rule: A plan is not execution. A build is not verification. A deployment is not acceptance. Missing evidence is UNVERIFIED, not PASS.

End-to-end lifecycle

Every asset and change follows the same controlled lifecycle. Recovery is a governed loop, not an untracked shortcut.

01 · DISCOVERInventory assets, owners, purpose and dependencies.
02 · RECONCILECompare source, provider, runtime and last-known-good.
03 · PLANDefine scope, risk, success, abort and rollback.
04 · AUTHORIZEAuthenticate, evaluate policy and record approvals.
05 · EXECUTERun bounded, reversible, traceable tasks.
06 · VERIFYRun independent tests and capture provenance.
07 · ACCEPTRecord owner and independent reviewer decision.
08 · DISTRIBUTESign and publish immutable approved artifacts.
09 · DELIVERPromote with release approval and rollback.
10 · OPERATEMonitor, support, maintain and improve.

Authentication, access & approval

Identity, permission, approval and acceptance are separate controls.

ControlRequired behavior
AuthenticationUnique identity, MFA for privileged roles, verified issuer/audience, secure session and revocation.
AuthorizationDeny-by-default RBAC + contextual ABAC; check principal, action, resource, tenant, environment and expiry.
ApprovalNamed authorized approver, exact scope, rationale, expiry and separation of duties.
ExecutionServer-side policy recheck, scoped credential, idempotency, audit trail and rollback.
AcceptanceIndependent reviewer checks evidence and records ACCEPTED or REJECTED.
ReleaseSeparate explicit production authorization for a named immutable artifact and environment.

Documentation, training & memory

Every operator needs role-based training, controlled runbooks, competency checks and refresh dates. Project knowledge, personal memory, operational context, customer data and evidence must remain separate, permission-checked and purpose-limited.

  • All users: identity, MFA, privacy and incident reporting.
  • Contributors: Git, review, CI, tests and evidence.
  • Operators: environments, runbooks, monitoring and change control.
  • Recovery: containment, restore, rollback and incident drills.
  • Verifiers: test design, provenance and independent review.
  • Release managers/admins: signing, authorization, access and audit.
