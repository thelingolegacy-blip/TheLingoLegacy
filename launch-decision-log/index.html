Launch Decision Log

Record go/no-go decisions for the non-billing launch wave so every change has a reason, owner, and rollback path.

Decision fields

  • Date/time
  • Decision
  • Reason
  • Owner
  • Rollback condition

Allowed decisions

  • Expand testers
  • Pause posts
  • Patch P0/P1
  • Rollback deployment

Not in scope

  • Billing activation
  • Subscription launch
  • Checkout promotion
