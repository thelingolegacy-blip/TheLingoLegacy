## Triage rules

1. Preserve source branches, commits, assets, and historical evidence until their unique value and provenance are understood.
2. Compare every PR head with its live base branch; classify ahead/behind/diverged and identify changed-file overlap before integration.
3. Treat draft state, mergeability, and green checks as separate facts. None alone proves acceptance.
4. Do not merge or close a PR solely because it is old, draft, or non-draft.
5. Group stacked PRs by dependency and reconcile parent branches before children.
6. Require full tests, hosted workflow jobs/steps/logs/artifacts, independent review, and product-specific acceptance before integration.
7. Keep G02 and production promotion protected. Do not dispatch runner workflows or deploy production as part of this inventory.
8. Preserve no-Vercel and no-real-money-game constraints; do not reactivate payments, wagering, or sweepstakes without their separate legal and release approvals.

## Scope limitations

This register records PR metadata only. It does not certify file contents, branch freshness, CI status, tests, deployments, security, legal compliance, or product completion. Those require per-PR and per-system verification.
