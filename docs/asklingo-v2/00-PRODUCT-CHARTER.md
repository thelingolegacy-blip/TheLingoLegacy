# askLINGO v2.0 — Product Charter

## Mission

Make askLINGO the trusted intelligence and orchestration interface for The Lingo Legacy ecosystem: a coherent way to understand, discover, create, plan, and coordinate work across connected products while preserving product boundaries and human authority.

## Product identity

- Product name: `askLINGO`
- Version: `2.0`
- Parent ecosystem: The Lingo Legacy
- Intelligence family: LINGOai
- Execution coordination: LINGOassist
- Primary promise: understand the request, identify the right context and capability, and return a useful, truthful, permission-aware result.

Do not append a star symbol to the product name. Do not position askLINGO as a clone of another named assistant.

## Outcomes

1. One coherent assistant experience across web, mobile, voice, and approved embedded surfaces.
2. Context-aware assistance across LINGO products without collapsing their data or permission boundaries.
3. Specialist agents that can research, design, analyze, and prepare bounded work.
4. Controlled execution through LINGOassist workers and approved connectors.
5. Verifiable approvals, actions, evidence, and operational state.
6. Preservation and reuse of existing assets before replacement or rebuild.

## Primary user journeys

- Ask a question and receive a clear answer with uncertainty stated.
- Research a product, project, or ecosystem topic with current sources and citations.
- Create or refine a product brief, story, campaign, design, or technical artifact.
- Ask for operational status and receive evidence-backed state, not inferred completion.
- Request an action; receive a preview of scope, target, impact, and required approval.
- Review the action, approve it through an authorized channel, and inspect its result and audit trail.

## Modes

Initial user-facing modes: Chat, Research, Create, Learn, Help, Plan, and Govern.

Professional subject modes (for example law, medicine, emergency response, and public safety) are informational/educational experiences unless separately validated and governed. They do not imply professional licensure, diagnosis, command authority, or operational dispatch.

## Product principles

- Truth before confidence.
- Useful first; style second.
- Preserve before replace.
- One shared contract; multiple interfaces.
- Least privilege by default.
- Human approval for consequential transitions.
- No fabricated tool execution, evidence, citations, or completion.
- No hidden spend or unapproved data retention.
- Accessibility, privacy, and safety are product requirements.

## Out of scope for this foundation

- Selecting or activating a paid model provider.
- Creating production credentials or secrets.
- Enabling production chat, memory persistence, or agent actions.
- Changing production Worker bindings, routes, DNS, Firebase, payment, or account settings.
- Merging or deploying this branch.
- Claiming legal or regulatory certification.

## Success measures

Measure answer usefulness, source/citation quality, task completion with verified evidence, authorization correctness, denied unauthorized actions, latency, reliability, cost per successful task, privacy requests handled, and recovery performance. Metrics must be privacy-reviewed and collected only under an approved policy.
