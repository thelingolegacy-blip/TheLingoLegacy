# LINGO Revenue, Download & Registration Playbook

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-10-31 · **Status:** Monetization design; no ad network, payment rail or campaign is activated by this document.

## 1. Revenue portfolio

Potential revenue streams, subject to product fit and legal review:
- Clearly labeled display/video sponsorships and contextual ads.
- Brand partnerships and sponsored community campaigns.
- Retail and apparel sales through approved commerce integrations.
- Studio services, creative production and licensing.
- Optional subscriptions or memberships with clear recurring-price and cancellation terms.
- Event sponsorship and ticketing only with venue, safety, tax and local compliance review.
- Sweepstakes sponsorships only under campaign-specific counsel-approved rules.

Keep ad revenue, commerce revenue, donations, grants and sweepstakes prize budgets separately classified and reconciled. Never represent donations as tax-deductible unless the receiving entity and program qualify.

## 2. Advertising standards

- Label paid content as Advertisement, Sponsored or equivalent, adjacent to the placement.
- Maintain an ad registry: advertiser, creative, landing destination, dates, targeting basis, disclosures, approval, spend and performance.
- Separate ads from game controls, navigation, entry buttons, system warnings and purchase confirmation.
- No accidental-click layouts, disguised close buttons, fake system alerts, deceptive countdowns or ads targeted using sensitive data.
- Provide consent and preference controls as required by applicable privacy law.
- Apply age-appropriate advertising restrictions and do not profile children.
- Cap frequency and monitor complaints, invalid traffic, viewability and brand safety.
- Keep ads out of safety-critical, payment, account recovery and legal-rules flows.
- Measure incremental revenue and user impact; pause placements that degrade usability or trust.

## 3. Download and registration experience

- Product landing page clearly states product purpose, supported devices, age rating, accessibility, data use and current availability.
- Downloads come only from verified official distribution channels or signed first-party release artifacts.
- Show version, release date, file type, checksum/signature where appropriate and minimum requirements.
- Registration collects only necessary fields; explains purpose and retention; separates optional marketing consent from service consent.
- Verify email/phone only when required; offer account recovery and session/device management.
- Never ask users to send passwords or one-time codes to support.
- Provide account export, correction and deletion workflows where applicable.
- For mobile app stores, complete privacy disclosures, age rating, permissions, screenshots, review notes, support URL and release testing before submission.

## 4. Conversion funnel

Measure:
1. Qualified landing visits.
2. Product detail views.
3. Download initiation.
4. Successful install/first open where measurement is consented and supported.
5. Registration start.
6. Verified registration completion.
7. First meaningful action.
8. 7/30-day retention.
9. Revenue per active user and contribution margin.

Report cohort, platform, geography, source, denominator, consent basis and attribution window. Do not treat page views or installs as active users or revenue.

## 5. Financial controls

- Separate gross sales, refunds, taxes, fees, chargebacks, ad revenue and partner payouts.
- Use an auditable ledger for balances and settlements; never use client-side values as financial authority.
- Require dual approval for refunds, payout changes, prize disbursement and material accounting adjustments.
- Reconcile processor reports to ledger and bank statements on a defined cadence.
- Set fraud thresholds and manual review; preserve evidence and appeal path.
- No real-money wagering or conversion of game points into cash absent a separate legal, regulatory, licensing and technical program.

## 6. Launch gates

- Product owner and finance owner approve unit economics and pricing.
- Legal reviews ads, consumer terms, privacy, subscriptions, sponsorship and promotional rules.
- Security reviews registration, identity, payment integrations and account recovery.
- Accessibility and mobile QA pass.
- Analytics definitions and consent reviewed.
- Support scripts, refund/cancellation process and incident escalation ready.
- Independent staging acceptance and production change authorization recorded.
