# LINGO Real-World Community & Retail Pitch Framework

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-10-31 · **Status:** Reusable business-development framework; no partnership or funding commitment implied.

## 1. Purpose

Create consistent, credible proposals for real-world retail, community services, creative studios, hospitality, events, education, transportation, food access, housing and local partnerships in South Jersey and Philadelphia-area communities.

## 2. Pitch package

Every pitch should contain:
1. Cover and one-sentence value proposition.
2. Community need and evidence, with source/date.
3. LINGO entity and accountable operating owner.
4. Proposed service, product or physical experience.
5. Target participants/customers and accessibility approach.
6. Site/facility/vehicle/equipment requirements.
7. Staffing, training, safeguarding and security model.
8. Operating hours, capacity, service levels and workflow.
9. Partner responsibilities and decision rights.
10. Startup costs, recurring costs, revenue assumptions and funding ask.
11. Permits, insurance, zoning, tax, licensing and legal dependencies.
12. Data/privacy, safety and incident controls.
13. Community outcomes and measurable KPIs.
14. Milestones, risks, dependencies and next decision.
15. Appendices: floor plan, wireframe, menu/catalog, schedule, budget, letters of support and references.

## 3. Portfolio lanes

- **Retail and apparel:** LINGOlane design, promotions, apparel and laundry-cycle services.
- **Gaming and entertainment:** LINGOarcade and LINGOslots experiences, strictly separating free entertainment, sweepstakes and any regulated activity.
- **Studio and media:** LINGOstudios creative production, events, sound, graphics and distribution.
- **Hospitality:** LINGOtowers hotel concept, with hospitality operations, accessibility, security, fire/life safety and permits.
- **Community services:** LINGOcommunity initiatives, education, food security, donations and referrals with qualified partners.
- **Transportation and logistics:** scheduled transport, deliveries, events and fleet operations with appropriate licensing and insurance.
- **Food and dining:** future Clarissa Diner concept as a later-stage enterprise component; no site, permit or funding status is implied.
- **Housing and development:** LINGO community housing concepts, subject to feasibility, financing, zoning, environmental review and licensed professionals.

## 4. Business case discipline

Use scenario-based financials:
- Startup capital expenditure and working capital.
- Fixed and variable operating costs.
- Staffing assumptions, wages, benefits and coverage.
- Capacity, utilization, pricing and seasonality.
- Revenue by source; do not combine grants, donations, sponsorship and sales.
- Break-even analysis, cash runway and downside case.
- Community value and nonfinancial outcomes reported separately from revenue.

Label every number as verified quote, historical actual, estimate, assumption or target. Cite source and date. Never invent letters of support, partner interest, funding, permits, property control or customer demand.

## 5. Community accountability

- Engage residents and local organizations before finalizing service design.
- Provide accessible feedback and complaint channels.
- Publish eligibility, pricing and service boundaries.
- Protect participant dignity and personal data.
- Track service reach, wait times, satisfaction, outcomes and complaints.
- Use transparent selection criteria for grants, donations, jobs and program participation.
- Do not promise housing, jobs, meals, rides or benefits until capacity and funding are confirmed.

## 6. Pitch status labels

`IDEA → DISCOVERY → FEASIBILITY → PARTNER_REVIEW → FUNDING_REVIEW → PERMITS_AND_COMPLIANCE → PILOT_READY → PILOT → EVALUATED → SCALE_APPROVED`

A concept deck is not a signed agreement, financing commitment, permit, lease, construction authorization or operational launch.
