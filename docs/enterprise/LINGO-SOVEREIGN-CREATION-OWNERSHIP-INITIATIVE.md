# LINGO Sovereign Creation & Ownership Initiative (LSCOI)

**Status:** Proposed program charter; documentation-only; not production-authoritative  
**Parent:** The Lingo Legacy / `LINGOlegacy_MASTER`  
**Canonical source:** `thelingolegacy-blip/TheLingoLegacy`  
**Architecture alignment:** LEAILOC, LECS, LKIS, Enterprise Control Tower, Surface Matrix, Enterprise Registry and 14-layer reference architecture  
**Release posture:** Production HOLD; G02 FAIL / UNVERIFIED; no release authority granted by this charter

## 1. Mission and doctrine

Establish LINGO as an AI-first, vertically integrated creation, invention, engineering, production, publishing and commercial-services enterprise. LINGO develops and operates capabilities for its own divisions first, then commercializes proven capabilities through paid products, services, subscriptions, licensing, manufacturing and professional partnerships.

**LINGO BUILDS. LINGO OWNS. AI FIRST. OWNERSHIP ALWAYS.**

"Ownership" means rights that LINGO can establish through applicable law, valid contracts, licenses and provenance—not an assumption that possession, payment, AI generation or use of a provider transfers every right. Third-party and open-source rights must be respected. No copying of protected source code, confidential information, trademarks or proprietary designs to create a purported replacement.

LSCOI is a governed enterprise program, not a separate master control plane, identity system, registry, data platform or release authority. It extends the existing enterprise architecture and must not duplicate LECS, LKIS, the Surface Matrix, Enterprise Registry, Enterprise Control Tower or Release Center.

## 2. Outcomes

1. A reconciled portfolio of LINGO divisions, products, inventions, services and production capabilities.
2. Reusable, provider-independent LINGO architecture for AI orchestration, application creation, creative production, document workflows and digital/physical fabrication.
3. A traceable asset and rights lifecycle for software, designs, media, documents, datasets, product specifications, inventions and physical goods.
4. A repeatable research-to-commercialization pipeline with independent quality, security, legal and release gates.
5. Internal use before external commercialization; validated offerings are sold under clear terms rather than presumed to be free public utilities.
6. Reduced dependency risk through deliberate build/buy/integrate/replace/retire decisions.

## 3. Enterprise authority and architecture alignment

- **LINGO Master Control / LECS:** enterprise priorities, policy, risk classification, approvals and release governance.
- **LKIS:** versioned knowledge, source provenance, retrieval, lessons and learning records.

The LEAILOC 14-layer architecture is the reference architecture. LSCOI spans governance, divisions, identity, intelligence, application, integration, execution, data, infrastructure, security, observability, engineering, release/recovery and learning layers. It does not create another parallel stack.

## 5. Master intelligence and agent model

LINGO AI is the coordinating intelligence layer, not an unconstrained autonomous authority. Provider adapters should allow independent selection of commercial or lawfully licensed open models based on quality, capability, privacy, cost, latency, availability and licensing.

Specialized agent roles may include research, strategy, invention, architecture, engineering, creative production, fabrication planning, business analysis, ownership/provenance, security, testing and quality. Every agent must have:
- a named purpose and accountable human owner;
- scoped input/output contracts and data classification;
- least-privilege tool and repository permissions;
- approved model/provider and data-use policy;
- source citations and provenance for factual claims;
- evaluation criteria, logs and versioned prompts/configuration;
- escalation behavior for uncertainty, conflicts and prohibited actions;
- a defined autonomy tier and human approval boundary.

Agents may research, draft, design, generate, code, test in isolated environments and prepare change proposals within their granted scope. They may not self-approve high-risk work, sign contracts, perform regulated professional acts, move funds, activate payments, weaken security, register or activate protected runners, change production DNS/routes, merge protected releases or promote production without the required human approvals.

## 6. Creation and ownership lifecycle

Every material creation follows this lifecycle:

1. **Discover:** problem statement, intended users, market and existing solutions.
2. **Research:** source inventory, evidence quality, competitive landscape and constraints.
3. **Invent/specify:** original concept, requirements, designs, architecture and invention disclosure.
4. **Rights intake:** creators, contributors, source materials, licenses, permissions and assignment needs.
5. **Architect:** reuse existing LINGO services; document dependencies, security and exit strategy.
6. **Create/fabricate:** build the digital or physical artifact with versioned inputs and work records.
7. **Test:** functional, quality, security, privacy, accessibility, safety and compliance tests as applicable.
8. **Integrate:** connect through versioned contracts and least-privilege interfaces.
9. **Accept:** independent review, evidence capture and accountable owner acceptance.
10. **Commercialize:** terms, pricing, licensing, customer support, fulfillment and financial tracking.
11. **Maintain/improve:** defects, revisions, customer feedback, cost, risk and lifecycle/retirement.

No asset is marked owned, verified, accepted, production-ready or live solely because it was generated, committed, purchased or visually demonstrated.

## 7. Canonical asset and rights record

Extend the existing Enterprise Registry / LKIS evidence model rather than creating a competing registry. Each asset record should carry, as applicable:

- stable asset ID, canonical title, asset type, division and product relationships;
- creator/contributor identities and role;
- creation, modification, intake and review timestamps;
- source repository/path/provider and immutable version or digest;
- parent/derived asset relationships and transformation history;
- source materials, provenance and third-party dependencies;
- rights classification: LINGO-owned, assignment-pending, licensed, open-source, public-domain, third-party restricted, or unknown;
- license/assignment/consent references, scope, territory, term and restrictions;
- commercial-use and distribution permissions;
- registrations or filing references where applicable;
- review status, reviewer, evidence and next review date;
- retention, archival, dispute and takedown handling.

Use explicit statuses such as `PROPOSED`, `RIGHTS_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `LICENSED`, `ASSIGNMENT_PENDING`, `OWNERSHIP_EVIDENCE_REVIEWED`, `RESTRICTED`, `DISPUTED` and `RETIRED`. Do not use "ownership verified" where the relevant legal evidence has not been reviewed.

## 8. Digital and physical production

### Digital fabrication

Includes websites, applications, APIs, software systems, games, workflows, documents, data products, media assets, AI agents and digital services. Require source control, reproducible builds, dependency/license inventory, secure development, tests, artifact provenance, accessibility and rollback.

### Physical fabrication

Includes apparel, consumer products, prototypes, fixtures, equipment and manufacturing. Require design files/specifications, bill of materials, supplier qualification, material provenance, safety and regulatory review, tolerances, quality inspections, acceptance tests, traceable revisions and recall/repair procedures. Electrical, structural, mechanical, medical, child-safety and other regulated work must be performed or approved by qualified professionals with required permits, testing and inspection.

## 9. Build / buy / integrate / replace / retire

Every material external dependency receives a decision record:
- **BUILD** where strategic differentiation, control, security, economics or ownership justifies the lifecycle cost.
- **BUY** where a commodity provider is demonstrably safer, cheaper or more reliable.
- **INTEGRATE** where provider capability is valuable but LINGO needs unified identity, workflow, audit or customer experience.
- **REPLACE** only after a LINGO-owned alternative passes feature, security, legal, reliability, migration and cost comparisons.
- **RETIRE** when redundant, unsupported, unsafe or uneconomic, after data/export, dependency and rollback review.

Record provider, purpose, owner, contract/license, data handling, cost/quota, criticality, failure mode, exit/migration plan and review date. Do not rebuild commodity cloud, foundation-model training or other capital-intensive infrastructure without a supported business case.

## 10. Shared technical standards

- GitHub canonical source; isolated branches and reviewable pull requests.
- Versioned APIs, schemas and event contracts; no undocumented dual-primary writes.
- Least-privilege identities, environment separation and secret references only.
- Provider-independent AI adapter interfaces and model evaluations.
- React/Next.js/TypeScript for web where suitable; Flutter for mobile; Unity or project-appropriate engine for games.
- Cloudflare as intended edge; Firebase and other data platforms only after actual project, ownership and environment reconciliation.
- Free-tier-first and $0 incremental spend unless separately approved.
- Accessible responsive interfaces, observability, backups, restore drills and documented rollback.
- No credentials, private keys, tokens or secret values in source, documents or agent context.

## 11. Commercialization and customer protection

Build and validate for internal LINGO use first. External revenue may come from subscriptions, SaaS, production packages, professional services, product sales, manufacturing, licensing, integrations, managed services and enterprise agreements. Pricing must account for provider costs, labor, materials, support, taxes, refunds, licensing, compliance and margin. No unvalidated price is a market commitment.

Customer contracts and product terms must define scope, service levels, data handling, ownership of pre-existing IP, newly commissioned work, licenses, warranties, limitations, support, refunds, termination and export. Separate LINGO-owned background IP from customer deliverables and third-party components.

## 12. Governance and release gates

The program follows LECS and the enterprise release policy. Every work item records objective, owner, executor, risk, data classification, dependencies, exact source SHA, acceptance criteria, tests, evidence, approvals and rollback.

Status definitions:
- `PLANNED`: requirements recorded.
- `BUILT`: implementation exists.
- `TESTED`: specified tests executed.
- `VERIFIED`: evidence independently reviewed.
- `CONNECTED`: integration works under its contract.
- `AUTHORIZED`: required approval recorded.
- `LIVE`: approved production operation confirmed.

Missing, stale, contradictory or inaccessible evidence is UNKNOWN/BLOCKED, not PASS. CI startup failures and zero-job runs are not successful tests.
