How the demo works.

01 · Layouts

Both layouts use five reels. Classic has three rows and 20 fixed paylines. Expanded has five rows and 25 fixed paylines.

02 · Winning lines

A line wins when the same symbol appears on at least three consecutive reels, starting from the leftmost reel, following that line’s row path.

03 · Demo payouts

Each winning line pays 2×, 4×, or 8× its selected demo bet for three, four, or five matching symbols. Line payouts are added together.

Outcomes are generated locally for entertainment testing. This is not a certified random-number generator, regulated gambling product, prize promotion, or real-value wallet.
