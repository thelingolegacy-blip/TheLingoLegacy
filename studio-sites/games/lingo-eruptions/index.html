How to Play

  1. Press and drag across neighboring glyphs of the same type, or tap adjacent matching tiles.
  2. Connect at least three glyphs in one continuous path.
  3. Unleash the chain to clear tiles and earn points.
  4. Falling glyphs may create bonus cascades.

Rules & Player Safety

Build a connected path of three or more matching glyphs. Longer chains score more. Falling glyphs can create automatic horizontal or vertical cascades.

Timed mode lasts 90 seconds. Relaxed mode has no timer. Sound and motion are optional. Your score is local demo data and has no monetary value.
