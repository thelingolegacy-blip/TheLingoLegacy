# Per-title Art Briefs — Parallel Production Batch 01

All briefs are grounded in the corresponding `studio-sites/games/<id>.md` game specification. These are art direction briefs, not a claim that finished artwork has been produced, integrated, or accepted. Each title can proceed independently under Workstream C while sharing the collection art bible.

## Shared production rules

- Collection-level finish: Industrial Noir framing, obsidian/graphite foundations, metallic gold and royal purple accents where appropriate.
- Genre-specific color, silhouette, and emotional tone must remain distinct; do not force the same palette onto every title.
- No real-money wagering/cash-out cues. Rewards and score visuals remain clearly virtual.
- No rapid flashing or strobing; provide reduced-motion alternatives.
- Keep title lettering live where localization or responsive scaling requires it.
- Record rights/source, dimensions, alt text, hashes, and consumer references before marking assets verified.
