Shared systems

The engine room

Reusable platform capabilities are built once, governed centrally and connected to each realm through explicit contracts.

Identity & access

LINGO ID, authentication, account recovery, consent, role scopes and authorization boundaries. Provider and production identity connections remain gated.

Data, APIs & event delivery

Domain-owned data, versioned OpenAPI contracts, event schemas, idempotent consumers and traceable integrations.

Creation & asset systems

LINGOstudio, design systems, game and media pipelines, asset provenance, rights tracking and approval workflows.

Master Control & dispatch

Portfolio registry, service health, job routing, recovery playbooks, safe retries, incident records and auditable approvals.

Trust, security & privacy

LINGOshield, threat models, data minimization, abuse controls, security reviews and age-appropriate experiences.

Release & resilience

CI evidence, environment separation, backups, rollback, monitoring, disaster recovery and independently approved promotion.

World Builder & Simulation

Versioned world schemas, procedural generation, scene graphs, GIS overlays, deterministic simulation, asset rights and Unity/web runtime adapters.

Game Live Operations

Server-authoritative sessions, progression, matchmaking, events, anti-cheat, moderation, telemetry, incident response and content rollback.

Public directory only. No live payroll, property, guest, road, lighting or utility records are exposed. GIS and operational telemetry require approved, least-privilege access.

A directory is not an activation.

Destination pages, demos and staging applications are not automatically live-certified. LINGOlegacy_MASTER governs the portfolio; shared platform services are not separate public brands. Account creation, wallet transfers, redeemable rewards, payments, cash-out and real-money gameplay remain unavailable until their separate verification and approval requirements pass.
