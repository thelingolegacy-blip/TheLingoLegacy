The shared foundation

What connects it all

Shared engines give every realm a consistent foundation for identity, content, operations, security and growth—without pretending unfinished integrations are already live.

LINGOcore & Stack Engine

Shared service contracts, APIs, event delivery, environment controls, health checks, feature gates and reusable platform capabilities.

LINGOidentity & Universal Legacy Wallet

Planned identity, permissions and rewards architecture. Account, wallet, transfer and redemption capabilities remain gated until security and provider acceptance.

LINGOstudio & Creation Systems

Shared workflows for design, engineering, games, books, music, film, assets, approvals, rights and production evidence.

Master Control, Recovery & Dispatch

Portfolio registry, monitoring, incident routing, runbooks, safe retries, job assignment, audit trails and release evidence.

World Builder & Simulation

Versioned world schemas, procedural generation, scene graphs, GIS overlays, deterministic simulation, asset rights and Unity/web runtime adapters.

Game Live Operations

Server-authoritative sessions, progression, matchmaking, events, anti-cheat, moderation, telemetry, incident response and content rollback.
