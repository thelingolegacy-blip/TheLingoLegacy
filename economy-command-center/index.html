AI Balancer workflow

The first release should generate recommendations and alerts, not automatic economy mutations.

Pipeline
1. Ingest
Read aggregate metrics from approved services.
2. Score
Normalize signals and compute the stress index.
3. Recommend
Write admin-reviewable actions with confidence and rollback notes.
Guardrails
Blocked
Automatic wallet freezes, direct balance mutations, live price changes, and payout reductions.
Allowed
Recommendations, alerts, trend reports, and admin-approved remediation plans.
