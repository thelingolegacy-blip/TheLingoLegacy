Community Guidelines

Keep the live beta helpful, safe, and focused on feedback.

Do

  • Report bugs clearly
  • Respect testers
  • Protect children’s content
  • Share device details

Do not

  • Share private data
  • Post secrets
  • Promise checkout or subscriptions
  • Harass testers

Escalate

  • Security concerns
  • Unsafe content
  • Repeated abuse
  • Production blockers
