# LINGO Worldline

Five original games share one continuity layer. They borrow only broad genre mechanics—not characters, maps, stories, art, dialogue, music, trademarks, or level designs from third-party franchises.

## Slate
- Tricia's Escape — mythic puzzle-adventure
- Doughboy's Oasis — connected-digital-world action RPG
- Crazy Weasol's — surreal precision platformer
- Jerseyshore: The Undead — co-op survival action
- Silly in Philly: The Streets — open-city narrative sandbox

## Core fantasy
Every world is a different GREED to overcome. The player's question is always: **Will you sink or rise?**

The five games share identity, progression, cosmetics, achievements, and world consequences while retaining independent gameplay loops.
