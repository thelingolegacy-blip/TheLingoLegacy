14 / Mathematics & measurement

Operational math & analytics

Metrics must show units, measurement windows, source freshness and assumptions. Forecasts must include model version and uncertainty.

Availability

FORMULA
Uptime %
(Total time − unavailable time) ÷ total time × 100

Queue drain time

FORMULA
Backlog ÷ rate
Pending events divided by sustainable processing events per second

Capacity headroom

FORMULA
Headroom %
(Provisioned capacity − observed peak) ÷ provisioned capacity × 100
