# LINGOsheilds Terms of Service — Counsel Draft Template

**IMPORTANT:** This is a drafting template, not a legally effective agreement. It requires jurisdiction-specific legal review and execution/publication before use.

## 1. Service
Define LINGOsheilds, supported services, accounts, repositories, agents, integrations, and environments.

## 2. Eligibility and accounts
Define eligibility, account security, organization administration, authorized users, and credential responsibilities.

## 3. Acceptable use
Prohibit unlawful activity, abuse, credential theft, malware, unauthorized access, destructive automation, and attempts to bypass platform security.

## 4. Customer content
Define ownership, license needed to operate the service, repository content, model-processing boundaries, and deletion.

## 5. AI and agents
Define agent capabilities, customer authorization, model-provider dependencies, limitations, generated output, human review, and prohibited autonomous production actions.

## 6. Third-party integrations
Define GitHub, GitLab, cloud, model, package, and deployment integrations and their independent terms.

## 7. Security
Describe security controls without making unsupported absolute guarantees.

## 8. Availability and changes
Define maintenance, service changes, beta functionality, and incident handling.

## 9. Fees
Define subscriptions, usage, taxes, refunds, overages, and billing.

## 10. Intellectual property
Define platform IP, customer IP, feedback, third-party IP, and licenses.

## 11. Confidentiality
Define confidential information and permitted disclosures.

## 12. Warranties and disclaimers
Counsel should draft jurisdiction-specific provisions.

## 13. Liability
Counsel should draft limitations, exclusions, and applicable carve-outs.

## 14. Indemnification
Counsel should define scope and exclusions.

## 15. Suspension and termination
Define security suspension, policy violations, termination, data export, and deletion.
